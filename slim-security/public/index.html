How is Slim CCTV Security cheaper than big-name providers?

We keep overhead low by working directly with local technicians and sourcing the same commercial-grade equipment big brands use — without franchise fees or long-term contracts baked into the price.

No. Our packages are one-time installation costs. Professional monitoring, if you choose it, is month-to-month with no cancellation fees.

Most homes are completed in a single visit, typically 2-4 hours depending on the number of cameras and your home's wiring.

Yes — every system includes free mobile app access so you can view live footage, get motion alerts, and review recordings from anywhere.
