Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Slim Security ("Slim CCTV Security," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or request a quote for CCTV and security camera installation services.

Information we collect

When you submit the quote form on this site, we collect the information you provide directly, which currently includes:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your ZIP code
  • The number of cameras you're interested in, if selected

We also automatically collect limited technical information through analytics and advertising tools, such as your approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser type, pages viewed, and how you arrived at the site (for example, from a Google ad).

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your quote request and schedule an installation
  • To improve this website and our marketing, including measuring how well our Google Ads campaigns perform
  • To meet legal, accounting, or security obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google Ads conversion tracking and may use Google Analytics or similar tools to understand site traffic and measure ad performance. These tools may set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

How we share information

We share information with service providers who help us operate this business — for example, scheduling and communication tools, and Google (for advertising and analytics). We do not share your information with other third parties except as needed to fulfill your service request, comply with the law, or protect our legal rights.

Data retention

We keep quote request information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a customer, for the duration of our business relationship plus a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable state privacy law.
